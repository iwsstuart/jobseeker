Less a product than a tool at the moment, the "Job Seeker" is a personal tool that I will use to track interesting roles (and the skills and experience they value) at companies I am particularly interested in.

The problem this will solve (for me) is that I find it difficult to stay completely current on job openings at specific companies. Larger job aggregator sites (such as LinkedIn) are difficult to configure in the way I want, are too noisy and can be innaccurate or untrustworthy. I would rather get job information from companies' own job boards, when possible. But with more than a few companies of interest, it becomes difficult to regularly check for updates accross all of them.

The simplest version of the tool will require me to identify companies of interest, provide information on my experience and interests, as well as any other specific guidance on what to look for in a role. The tool will periodically check for interesting opportunities, and I will also be able to request updates on demand.

The user interface will be bare-bones. Utilizing an existing interface for inputing text and uploading attachments (e.g. WhatsApp) and for receiving messages may be sufficient.
