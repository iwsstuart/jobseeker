# Candidate Profile — Ian Stuart

## Background summary
8+ years as a data analytics practitioner, spanning digital identity (ID.me),
EV charging infrastructure (Volta Charging), and commercial real estate
(WeWork). Core strength is translating ambiguous business problems into
scalable data infrastructure and decision-making tools, working closely with
Product, Engineering, and Ops.

## Core skills (strong positive signal)
- SQL (advanced — CTEs, window functions, performance tuning, data quality testing)
- Python (pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Flask)
- dbt + Snowflake (data modeling, ELT pipeline design)
- BigQuery, PostgreSQL/PostGIS
- KPI definition, metrics frameworks, self-service reporting/BI (Tableau,
  Looker, Metabase, Streamlit)
- Forecasting, causal inference, regression, supervised/unsupervised ML
- AI-assisted development: using LLMs (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot) to
  accelerate analytics work, prototyping, and code generation
- Geospatial data and analysis (PostGIS, custom geospatial visualization)
- Cross-functional delivery: requirements gathering, stakeholder alignment,
  mentorship

## Role types of interest
Open to a range of titles that map to this skillset, since terminology
varies significantly by company. Treat all of these as potential matches,
not just exact title hits:
- Senior / Staff Data Analyst
- Analytics Engineer
- Data Scientist (applied, not purely research)
- Product Analyst / Product Data Scientist
- Data Engineer (especially analytics-adjacent, dbt/pipeline-focused)
- Roles explicitly focused on building or shaping AI/LLM-powered products
  or tools (not just using AI as a personal productivity aid) — this is a
  genuine area of interest, not just a skill to mention

## Seniority
Flexible. Individual contributor roles (Senior/Staff level) are the most
direct fit given experience, but management/team lead roles should also be
surfaced if they otherwise look like a strong fit. Do not filter out a role
purely because it includes some people-management scope.

## Domain interest
No hard domain requirement at this stage. Climate, sustainability, and
clean energy are areas of active interest (reflected in current target
company list), but this is a soft preference, not a filter — strong
non-domain roles should be surfaced too. Treat domain fit as one positive
signal among several, not a gate.

## What makes a role a strong match
- Heavy use of SQL and/or Python for analytics, data modeling, or pipeline work
- Ownership of ambiguous, cross-functional problems rather than narrowly
  scoped reporting tasks
- Opportunities to shape product or business strategy through data, not
  just produce dashboards on request
- Roles where dbt, Snowflake, or modern data stack tools are in use
- Roles building AI/LLM-powered products or internal tools

## What makes a role a weaker match (but not necessarily excluded)
- Roles that are purely dashboard/reporting maintenance with no modeling
  or pipeline ownership
- Roles requiring deep specialization outside this background (e.g.
  hardcore backend/infra engineering, pure research science) with no
  analytics component
- Sales, recruiting, or other clearly non-technical functions (these
  should be filtered out before reaching this evaluation step)

## Notes for the matching step
- Be generous, not strict — this profile is intentionally broad for v1.
  Err toward surfacing borderline matches with clear reasoning rather than
  silently excluding them.
- Always explain *why* a role is or isn't a match in plain terms, referencing
  specific aspects of the role description and this profile.
- Flag when a role is an interesting match for reasons outside the explicit
  domain list (e.g. strong technical fit in an unexpected industry).
